Using Turbo Notify responsibly keeps the service running and reduces the risk of a block, both on the platform and on WhatsApp. On this page you will find the main risks, the prohibited practices and what to do to avoid problems.

Turbo Notify is not the official WhatsApp Business API from Meta. Good practices and the sending pace the platform applies reduce the risk of your number being blocked, but never eliminate it.

## Turbo Notify

To keep the environment safe and reliable for everyone, Turbo Notify sets clear usage rules. Inappropriate conduct may lead to restrictions or to suspended access to the platform.

The following are considered abusive practices: use for illegal purposes, sending spam, fraud, attempts to get around technical limits, and other actions described in our <ExternalLink href="https://turbonotify.com/en/legal/terms-of-service">Terms of Use</ExternalLink>. These practices are not allowed.

If violations are found, the service may be suspended immediately and without prior notice, to protect the platform and its other users.

## WhatsApp

The <ExternalLink href="https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/business-policy/">WhatsApp policies</ExternalLink> apply to any number connected to Turbo Notify. WhatsApp's restrictions and usage rules apply to every account, including those that use the official Business API.

Whatever the integration method, sending unsolicited, bulk or annoying messages can lead recipients to report you and, as a result, to your number being blocked on WhatsApp.

## Recommendations

- Only send messages to contacts who agreed to receive them.
- Honor opt-out or block requests immediately.
- Avoid repeated messages, excessive promotions or communications that are not relevant to the recipient.
- Do not use contact lists bought from third parties or without proof of consent.
- Do not share sensitive or personal information without authorization.
- Do not use the platform for illegal or fraudulent purposes, or in ways that violate the rights of others.
- Always follow the Turbo Notify <ExternalLink href="https://turbonotify.com/en/legal/terms-of-service">Terms of Use</ExternalLink> and the <ExternalLink href="https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/business-policy/">WhatsApp policies</ExternalLink>.

## Rate limits by plan

On top of the conduct recommendations, Turbo Notify enforces a minimum spacing between sends that scales with your plan: from 10 sends per minute on the Free plan to 150 sends per minute on Custom. This pace reduces the risk of WhatsApp treating your number as spam. See the full table by plan and the related error codes in [Rate limits](/en/messages/rate-limits/).

## Blocks and responsibility

If WhatsApp blocks your number, Turbo Notify has no responsibility or obligation to intervene, handle or request the unblock with WhatsApp.

Managing and eventually recovering the number is entirely the responsibility of the number's owner. To avoid trouble, always use the platform as recommended.

## Final considerations

Turbo Notify spaces out sends to reduce the risk of a block, but it cannot prevent your number from being blocked, especially if recipients complain or report you.

That is why you should respect the applicable law, the <ExternalLink href="https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/business-policy/">WhatsApp policies</ExternalLink> and the <ExternalLink href="https://turbonotify.com/en/legal/terms-of-service">Terms of Service</ExternalLink> to keep the service running.
