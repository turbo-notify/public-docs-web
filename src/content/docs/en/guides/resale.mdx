If your platform (a CRM, a booking system, an AI agent) wants to offer WhatsApp to its own customers, you can do it with a single Turbo Notify account: each end customer gets an **extra number** in your account, paired from their own phone, inside your screen. You pay Turbo Notify a fixed amount per number and charge your customers whatever you want.

## How it works

| Step | Who | How |
|---|---|---|
| Add the customer's number | Your platform | `POST /v1/numbers` with the customer's `external_id` |
| Pair | The customer, on their phone | Types the code your screen shows |
| Send and receive | Your platform | REST API or MCP server on the number's alias, events on your webhook |
| Bill the customer | Your platform | Your price |
| Remove | Your platform | `DELETE /v1/numbers/{alias}` |

## What you need

- **The Business plan (`BUSINESS`).** Extra numbers exist on the Business and Custom plans. An account holds up to **1,000** extra numbers; for more, talk to sales@turbonotify.com about the Custom plan.
- **Your main number connected.** An extra number can only be paired while the account's main number is connected; without it, pairing fails with `reason: "main_number_not_connected"`. Connect it as in the [Quickstart](/en/guides/quickstart/#2-connect-your-main-number) and watch it (`number.disconnected` and `number.revoked` with `alias: "main"`).
- **A key with the Resale template.** Under <DashboardLink path="/api-keys">API Keys</DashboardLink>, the **Resale** template selects `numbers:read`, `numbers:write`, `messages:send`, `messages:read`, `contacts:read` and `events:read`. The key stays on your platform's server; never hand it to an end customer. See [API key](/en/general/access-key/).

## What it costs

Each extra number costs <Price item="extraNumber" /> per month, on top of the Business plan (<Price item="businessPlan" /> per month). Prices show in your account's currency: Brazilian real for accounts in Brazil, US dollar for every other country. Each extra number has the plan's full allowance, which on Business is 50,000 messages per cycle, and shares no quota with the other numbers.

You decide how much to charge each customer. Turbo Notify takes no commission on your sale.

When you add a number mid-cycle, the account pays right away the amount proportional to the days left; from the next cycle on, the number is billed at the full price on the invoice. The number is only created if that charge is approved. Details in [Extra number billing](/en/numbers/billing/).

## 1. Add one number per customer

Use `external_id` to store the customer's identifier in your system. It is yours, it does not change when the alias changes, and it comes back in every lookup and in every `number.*` event:

```bash
curl -X POST https://api.turbonotify.com/v1/numbers \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "phone_number": "+5511988887777",
    "alias": "customer_4821",
    "external_id": "4821",
    "description": "Ana Souza Clinic"
  }'
```

The same example in Node, Python, Go, PHP, C# and Java is in [Add extra numbers](/en/numbers/add/).

- `alias` addresses the number in every route (`/v1/numbers/customer_4821/messages`). It starts with a letter and has up to 50 characters.
- `external_id` has 1 to 128 characters and is unique among the account's numbers. Reusing one already in use answers `409 external_id_already_used`.
- The number starts with `status: "pending"`.

Each creation is a real card charge, so the pace of creations per account is limited: above it, the response is `429 number_creation_rate_limited` with a `Retry-After` header, and nothing is charged. Details in [Add extra numbers](/en/numbers/add/).

## 2. Pair from the customer's phone

The customer completes pairing alone, on your screen, without talking to you:

1. Request the code with `POST /v1/numbers/customer_4821/pair`.
2. Call `GET /v1/numbers/customer_4821` every 2 seconds. When the `pairing` block is filled in, show `pairing.data` (the code, such as `Z5X7-LTYL`) and a countdown to `pairing.expires_at`.
3. Show the customer the path on the phone: **WhatsApp > Linked devices > Link a device > Link with phone number instead**, then type the code.
4. Keep **Generate a new code** always visible. A mistyped code is invalidated by WhatsApp on the spot, without telling anyone; the button calls `POST .../pair` again and the new code replaces the previous one.
5. Finish on the result: `status: "connected"` is success; `status: "failed"` carries the reason in `reason`.

Code requests are limited per number and per account, so that WhatsApp itself does not block new codes. On a `429 pairing_rate_limited`, show the time from the `Retry-After` header instead of retrying. The full walkthrough, with examples, is in [Pairing](/en/numbers/pairing/#recommended-pairing-experience).

Once paired, the number shows under **Linked devices** on the customer's phone with your organization's name.

### What to tell the customer when it fails

| `reason` | Message for the customer |
|---|---|
| `pairing_timeout` | The code expired before it was used. Generate a new code and type it right away. |
| `paired_phone_mismatch` | The code was typed on another WhatsApp. Use the phone of the registered number. |
| `phone_already_bound` | This number is already in use. Contact the platform's support. |
| `main_number_not_connected` | We are having a temporary issue. Try again in a few minutes. (For you: reconnect the main number.) |
| `code_generation_failed` | WhatsApp did not generate the code just now. Try again in a moment. |
| `connection_lost` | The connection dropped during pairing. Pair again. |
| `pairing_failed` | Pairing could not be completed. Try again. |

Treat a `reason` you do not know as `pairing_failed`. The same reason arrives in the `number.connect.failed` webhook.

## 3. One webhook for every customer

Register a single account webhook under <DashboardLink path="/webhooks">Webhooks</DashboardLink>. It receives the events of every number, and each event says which number it came from:

- `number.*` events (connected, disconnected, revoked, pairing failure, removal) carry `data.external_id`: route them straight to the right customer.
- Message events carry `number_alias` in the envelope. Store the alias and `external_id` pair when you add the number, or call `GET /v1/numbers?external_id=4821`.

To watch your customer base, `GET /v1/numbers?status=failed` or `?status=disconnected` lists the numbers that need attention. When a number is revoked on the phone (`number.revoked`), tell the customer and offer to pair again.

## 4. Send and receive on the customer's behalf

Every message, contact and group route sits under the number's alias: `POST /v1/numbers/customer_4821/messages`. Through the [MCP server](/en/general/mcp-server/), tools take `number_alias`. Quotas belong to each number, so one customer's usage never consumes another's allowance.

## 5. Remove when the customer leaves

1. Disconnect: `POST /v1/numbers/customer_4821/disconnect`.
2. Remove: `DELETE /v1/numbers/customer_4821`.

If you remove a number right after adding it, before using it, the creation charge may be refunded automatically. Otherwise the number stays active until the end of the cycle already paid and is removed afterwards; until then, the removal can be canceled. The `DELETE` response tells you what happened (`refund_eligible`, `refund_amount_cents`, `refund_status`, `scheduled_deletion_at`). Refund questions: billing@turbonotify.com. See [Remove](/en/numbers/remove/).

## Risk of blocks

Turbo Notify connects each number as a linked device; it does not use the official WhatsApp Business API from Meta. The platform's sending pace reduces the risk of a block but does not remove it, and the decision is WhatsApp's. Advise your customers to:

- Message only people who know the number and agreed to receive messages.
- Start slowly with a new number; Turbo Notify limits the volume of the first days.
- Avoid sending the same message to many recipients in a row.

The full practices are in [Avoid blocks](/en/general/abuse/).
