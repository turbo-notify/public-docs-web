**Date:** 2026-06-21
**Status:** Applied

## What changed

- **`at` = the real WhatsApp action moment** for **outbound** message events
  (`message.sent`/`replied`/`edited`/`deleted` and the `*.failed` ones) and for the
  real-action `number.*` events (`number.connected`/`disconnected`/`revoked`/`connect.failed`).
  Previously their `at` was ~equal to `created_at`; it now carries the genuine
  WhatsApp timestamp and is typically **earlier** than `created_at` (when Turbo
  Notify emitted the event). `created_at` and `at` are **distinct values**.
- **Three events no longer carry `at`**: `contact.picture_changed`,
  `group.picture_changed`, and `group.joined` — these are picture/snapshot
  discoveries with no single WhatsApp action moment. Only the envelope
  `created_at` applies.
- **All group lifecycle webhooks are now delivered**: `group.name_changed`,
  `group.description_changed`, `group.settings_changed`, `group.participants_added`,
  `group.participants_removed`, `group.participants_promoted`,
  `group.participants_demoted`, `group.joined`, `group.left` (in addition to the
  already-existing `group.picture_changed`).
- **`GET /v1/numbers/{alias}/messages/{id}`**: the **top-level** `at` is the message's **stable
  occurrence moment** (outbound: the real send; inbound: the receive) and does
  **not** change as the message advances `sent → delivered → read`. The nested
  `status.at` is the **current delivery-status** timestamp and **advances** with
  each transition.

## Compatibility

Pre-production. The public-contract field **names** are unchanged (`at`,
`created_at`, `status.at`); only the **source** of the values was corrected.
Clients that treat `at` as the action moment and `created_at` as the emission
moment need no changes.
