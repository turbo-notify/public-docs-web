<CardGrid>
  <Card title="WhatsApp API" icon="rocket">

:::note[Not the official Meta API]
Turbo Notify connects your WhatsApp number as a linked device, like WhatsApp Web. It is not the official WhatsApp Business API. The platform's sending pace reduces the risk of your number being blocked, but does not remove it: see [Avoid blocks](/en/general/abuse/).
:::
